# FlashFocus Support

Find an answer below, or reach out by email.

---

## Quick Help

### Studying

- **Where do I find decks to study?**
  Open the **Store** tab to browse curated decks by category, then tap **Save** to add one to *My decks*. You can also create your own from the **+** button on the Decks tab.

- **What card types are supported?**
  Three: **Flip** (classic Q&A), **Cloze** (fill-in-the-blank), and **Multiple Choice**. You can mix card types within a single deck.

- **What study modes are there?**
  *Play* (flip-through), *Quiz* (scored self-test), *SRS review* (only the cards due today), and *Exam mode* (timed, with a final score report).

### AI Features

- **How do I generate a deck with AI?**
  On the Decks tab, tap **+** then **Create with AI**. Choose a topic, paste text, upload a PDF or Word/PowerPoint file, take a photo of your notes, or paste a URL. Each generation costs Flash Credits; the cost is shown before you confirm.

- **What is *Explain this card*?**
  When you flip a card to the answer, tap *Explain* for a tutor-style breakdown of why the answer is what it is. Costs 1 Flash Credit.

- **How does *Chat with deck* work?**
  Open a deck's detail screen and tap *Chat*. You can ask questions about the material, ask the AI to quiz you, or get hints. Each message costs 1 Flash Credit (or is included in your Pro allowance).

- **Why was an answer flagged as possibly wrong?**
  AI explanations occasionally flag a card's answer as incorrect when it's actually fine — particularly with class-specific terminology. Treat AI output as a study aid, not the source of truth.

### Flash Credits & Pro

- **How do I get more Flash Credits?**
  Three ways: buy a credit pack from the wallet (Apple App Store / Google Play), refer a friend (15 credits to each of you when they sign up and study), or wait for the monthly refresh on the 1st of the month (5 credits, free).

- **A purchase didn't add credits to my balance.**
  Open *More → Wallet* and pull-to-refresh. If credits are still missing after a few minutes, email us with the receipt and we'll investigate.

- **How do I cancel a Pro subscription?**
  - **iOS:** *Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → FlashFocus*
  - **Android:** *Play Store → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → FlashFocus*

  Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

- **What does Pro include?**
  Unlimited AI features without spending Flash Credits, plus a generous monthly chat allowance (with a soft cap shown on the paywall).

### Account & Data

- **How do I delete my account?**
  *More → Security → Delete my account*. Your decks, study history, Flash Credits balance, and subscription record are removed within 30 days. Cancel any active App Store / Play Store subscription separately.

- **How do I download my data?**
  *More → Security → Download my data*. You'll receive a JSON archive by email.

- **How do I move my decks to a new phone?**
  *More → Backup & restore → Back up my decks* exports a `.flashfocus` file. Share it to your new device (AirDrop, email, cloud), then *Backup & restore → Restore from a backup* on the new device. Free for everyone, your own decks only.

- **Can I send a backup to a friend?**
  No. Backup files are scoped to the account that created them, so they can't be used to share decks across accounts.

### Notifications

- **I'm not getting study reminders.**
  Make sure notifications are enabled for FlashFocus in your device settings, and that you've set your study times in *More → Notification settings*.

- **Reminders come at the wrong time.**
  Reminders use your device's local time zone. Update your study times in-app if your routine has changed.

## Troubleshooting

- **App won't open:** Force-quit the app, then reopen. If that fails, restart the device. As a last resort, reinstall — your decks are safe if you've created a backup; cloud-synced data on Pro accounts will return after sign-in.
- **AI feature failed:** If credits were deducted for a failed AI call, they're refunded automatically. If they aren't back within a minute, pull-to-refresh on the wallet.
- **Sign-in with Google / Apple doesn't work:** Make sure your device is online and signed into the corresponding account. If it still fails, try email + password as a fallback.
